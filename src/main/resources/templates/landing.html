Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to donate blood?
Generally, individuals who are healthy, at least 17 years old, weigh at least 45kg (110 lbs), and haven't donated in the last 56 days are eligible.
How does PulseLink help in emergencies?
PulseLink allows patients and hospitals to create emergency blood requests that notify all registered eligible matching donors instantly.
Are donation records stored securely?
Yes, all patient and donor records are stored in a fully secured, role-authenticated database system built using Spring Security.
